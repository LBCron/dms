!!! tip "Can't find an area in the sidebar?"
    Some areas of the workbench, for example *Maintenance mode*, *Database connection* or *Maintenance operations*, are hidden in the template *Household*. At the bottom of the sidebar it says how many areas are hidden; one click shows them. You can show them permanently under [Customise the workbench](https://help.ablago.app/admin/aktuell/en/uebersicht/#werkbank-anpassen). **Hidden does not mean switched off.**
