# Keep or destroy the original? {#original-behalten-oder-vernichten}

You have scanned a paper and filed it in Ablago. May the original go now? Often yes, sometimes better not. This page helps you decide.

!!! warning "No legal or tax advice"
    These are rules of thumb for a private household, based on the law in **Germany**; other countries have their own rules and periods. In individual cases, especially with disputes, inheritance, tax or a business, please ask an advice centre, your tax adviser or a lawyer.

![Keep the original, a scan is usually enough, caution](grafiken/original-behalten.en.svg){ .grafik }

## The rule of thumb {#die-faustregel}

**Keep the original if the paper itself proves something or has a value.** Otherwise a scan is usually enough.

Why? In a household it is a simple trade-off:

- **Keeping costs almost nothing.** A household collects about a shoebox of paper a year. It stands in the corner and bothers nobody.
- **If the original is missing, that can be a disadvantage in the rare case of a dispute** – for instance with a guarantee, a tradesperson or an inheritance.

So the rule is: **When in doubt, keep it.** With the [box per year](#die-kiste-je-jahr) it is no effort.

## Keep the original {#original-behalten}

- Certificates (birth, marriage, divorce) and identity documents
- Notarised contracts and documents about a house, flat or plot of land
- Powers of attorney, advance healthcare directive, care directive
- Will, ideally in official custody at the probate court
- Vehicle registration document (registration certificate part II)
- Savings books and securities
- Judgments and enforceable titles
- Signed contracts that could be disputed
- Life and pension insurance policies, also after payout
- Pension documents (employment contracts, social insurance records) until the pension notice has arrived

**Why a scan isn't enough here:** in court, a scan is not a document in the legal sense. Anyone who wants to prove something with a signed paper must in principle present the original in a dispute ([§ 420 ZPO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/zpo/__420.html)). A scan is then only "freely assessed", and in case of doubt that is worth less.

## A scan is usually enough {#scan-genugt-meist}

- Bank statements
- Payslips, once you have checked them against your account
- Ordinary invoices and receipts. Keep them for at least three years, counted from the end of the year. That way you can fend off an unjustified reminder.
- Till receipts. They are often on thermal paper and **fade**. Here the scan is even better than the original.
- Doctors' bills
- Correspondence, letters, notices
- Operating instructions
- Receipts for the tax return. You no longer submit them, but must be able to present them if the tax office asks. As a rule, a good scan is enough for that.

## Caution {#achtung}

- **Invoices from tradespeople for work on a house or flat** must be kept by private individuals for **two years** ([§ 14b UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__14b.html)). The period begins at the end of the year in which the invoice was issued. The invoice, a payment record or another conclusive document is allowed. So file **the scan and the bank statement together** in the same Mappe. For construction defects, a limitation period of five years usually applies. The state archives therefore recommend that owners and private landlords keep tradespeople's invoices for **six years**. With the [box per year](#die-kiste-je-jahr) that costs hardly anything.
- **Guarantee and warranty:** some manufacturers require the **original receipt** for the guarantee. Keep it for at least two years for the statutory warranty, and correspondingly longer for a longer guarantee.
- **Check the backup before destroying anything.** A scan is only useful if it isn't lost with the hard disk. Ask your administrator whether [Ablago's backup](https://help.ablago.app/admin/aktuell/en/sicherung/) is running and when it was last checked.
- **Readable for a long time:** if there is an [archive version (PDF/A)](dokumente.md#archivfassung-pdfa) of a document, any program will still open it in many years. And under [Export my data](profil.md#meine-daten-exportieren) you can get a copy in open formats at any time, which you can read without Ablago.

## Even if you keep the original – Ablago helps { #auch-wenn-du-das-original-behaltst-das-dms-hilft }

### Lock originals away safely {#originale-sicher-wegschlieen}

What has to stay in the original – certificates, passport, notarised contracts, vehicle registration document, savings books – can go into a **bank safe deposit box** or a fireproof document safe. There it is protected against fire, water and burglary. You can still read the content in Ablago at any time.

- What you need every day, for example your identity card and driving licence, of course stays at home.
- **Not in the safe deposit box** belongs what is needed immediately in an emergency: lasting power of attorney, advance healthcare directive, care directive. The safe deposit box can only be reached during the bank's opening hours, and not at all without a power of attorney.
- Ask your bank how far the content of the safe deposit box is insured. The cover is often limited.

### The box per year {#die-kiste-je-jahr}

For papers you only have to keep for a while, you don't need a binder. These are, for example, tradespeople's invoices (two years) or purchase receipts for the warranty.

1. Scan them and file them in Ablago.
2. Simply put the paper in order into a **box with the year on it**, for example "Box 2026".
3. Once the period for everything in it is over, the box goes as a whole. Under the statutory period, tradespeople's invoices from "Box 2026" could therefore go at the end of 2028; if you follow the archives' recommendation, wait until the end of 2032. Please shred them or take them to a document destruction service, because they contain personal data.

If you do need an original after all, you'll find it in Ablago. The date tells you which box it is in.

### Recording where the original is {#festhalten-wo-das-original-liegt}

Every Ablago installation has the view **Storage location**. A storage location is a small Mappe for a place where paper is kept – and it can be inside another one: "at home › study cupboard › red binder", "Box 2026", "bank safe deposit box", "at the notary's" or simply "destroyed".

1. In the view *Storage location*, create a location with **+ New**: enter the *Name* and, under *Located in*, choose the location it is in (e.g. the cupboard for the binder). At the very top, *Located in* stays empty.
2. In the Mappe whose paper you keep, choose the location in the field **Whereabouts of original documents** – if your administrator has set up the field.
3. If the binder moves, you only change *Located in* on the binder. All Mappen in it then show the new location.

At the storage location itself, the tab **Kept here** shows which Mappen are there, and **Contains** which locations are inside it.

## For the self-employed and businesses {#fur-selbstandige-und-unternehmen}

!!! note "Stricter rules apply here"
    If you run a business or work freelance, in Germany you usually have to keep receipts for **eight or ten years** and may only destroy paper according to a **defined procedure** (GoBD). With many receipts, it is then no longer about the single sheet but about the procedure: who scans, how is it checked, when is it destroyed? Large companies often follow the BSI's technical guideline *TR-RESISCAN* for this.

    In Ablago, the [archive mode](https://help.ablago.app/admin/aktuell/en/archiv/) and the [procedure documentation template](https://help.ablago.app/admin/aktuell/en/verfahrensdokumentation/) help with this. In any case, discuss the procedure with your tax adviser.
