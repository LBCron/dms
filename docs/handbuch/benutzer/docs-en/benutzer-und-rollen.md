# Users and roles {#benutzer-und-rollen}

The people who use Ablago are Mappen too. That way a Mappe can show **who takes care of something** – for example, who keeps an eye on the notice period of a contract.

## Users {#benutzer}

In the view **Users**, every user has their Mappe. Name, e-mail address, roles and whether the account is active are shown there – for reading. They are changed in your own [profile](profil.md) or by an administrator.

![A user's Mappe](bilder/benutzer-mappe.png)

If an administrator has added further fields (such as a phone number), you can fill them in like in any other Mappe.

User Mappen are not created in the filing view and not deleted there either – that happens via user management. Anyone who no longer uses Ablago is deactivated; their Mappe is kept, and their name then appears in italics and struck through everywhere. That way it remains recognisable who used to be responsible for something.

## Roles {#rollen}

A **role** groups users who are responsible for something – for example "Parents", "Taxes and finances" or "House and garden". Roles are also Mappen, in the view **Role**.

![A role with its users](bilder/rolle.png)

- **Create:** in the view *Role*, click **+ New**, enter a name, save. The name must be unique. If **+ New** is missing, an administrator has restricted creating roles.
- **Change and delete** like any other Mappe. If users have the role, they lose it when it is deleted. If a field still links to the role (such as "responsible role" in a contract), it can only be deleted once that link is removed.
- **Who has a role** is shown on the role's tab *Users*. Only an administrator can assign it.

**Administrator** and **Member** are the **system roles**: they result from the user's account and can be neither renamed nor deleted.

!!! info "Roles and permissions"
    A role does not give any permissions by itself. An administrator can, however, set in the permissions what a role may do – for example, that only the "Parents" see the payslips. What applies to you is shown under My account in [My permissions](profil.md#meine-rechte).

    Two permissions are often confused: **Read (R) means viewing** – opening Mappen and documents in Ablago. **Export (E) means taking along** – downloading, sharing, sending by e-mail, exporting and opening via the [network drive](laufwerk.md). So a role may be allowed to view paperwork without being able to take it along (see [Without the export permission](dokumente.md#ohne-exportrecht)). Saving while viewing can never be prevented entirely (browser, screenshot).

## Entering who is responsible {#verantwortliche-eintragen}

If a Mappe type has a field like **Responsible**, you choose a user there or – in a separate field – a role, just like with any other [link](mappen.md#auf-andere-mappen-verweisen).

The user's or role's Mappe then lists under *Links* all Mappen they are responsible for.
