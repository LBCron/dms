# What is a DMS for? {#wofur-ein-dms}

"I never forget an invoice, and I always cancel my contracts on time." Quite possibly that's true. This page doesn't want to convince you otherwise or scare you. It shows **when** a DMS makes a difference – and invites you to a small self-check.

## Why you often don't feel the problem {#warum-man-das-problem-oft-nicht-spurt}

For most people, one single person is everything at once: they file, search, pay, cancel and carry the responsibility. The knowledge sits in **one head** – and as long as that stays so, it often works surprisingly well.

![Four situations in which the knowledge in one head is no longer enough](grafiken/kippen.en.svg){ .grafik }

It only becomes noticeable when one of these conditions tips over:

- **Another person** has to find or decide something – your partner, the children, you for your parents. Or you yourself can't be reached for once.
- **A lot of time passes** – the guarantee after two years, the tradesperson's invoice when selling the house after fifteen.
- **It becomes more** – your own home, letting, family, self-employment.
- **Something happens** – water damage, a move, a separation, the tax return. Then you need a lot at once.

## What you pay – and what you get for it {#was-du-bezahlst-und-was-du-dafur-bekommst}

Banking and contract apps are handy: they show where your money goes and recognise ongoing contracts from the direct debits. Ablago doesn't replace them – it takes care of the other half.

![Banking and contract apps show what you pay; Ablago records what you get for it](grafiken/bezahlen-bekommen.en.svg){ .grafik }

An app only sees a contract once money flows. But much of what counts in everyday life costs nothing at all or only once:

- **Rights:** guarantee, statutory warranty, exactly what your insurance covers, cancellation period.
- **Proof:** purchase contract and vehicle registration, energy performance certificate, school reports, certificates, vaccination record, powers of attorney.
- **Conditions:** notice period, minimum term, excess, customer number, contact person.
- **Things:** which boiler is in the basement, when it was serviced and by whom.

In short: **the bank statement shows what you pay. The binder shows what you get for it** – and lets you prove something, claim it or hand it over to someone else.

## Not loose files, but connections {#nicht-lose-dateien-sondern-zusammenhange}

Ablago is more than a folder full of PDFs. In Ablago things are connected, as in real life: the house has a boiler, the boiler has a maintenance contract and an invoice from the installation, the invoice has a specialist firm.

![House, boiler, maintenance contract, invoice and specialist firm are connected with each other](grafiken/zusammenhang.en.svg){ .grafik }

When the specialist firm calls, one click shows you everything you have had to do with them. When the boiler breaks down, you find the instructions, maintenance contract and guarantee in one place. What this is called in Ablago is described under [The idea: folders that know what's in them](index.md#die-idee-mappen-statt-ordner).

## What you get from it – without any emergency {#was-du-davon-hast-ganz-ohne-notfall}

![A clear head, time, using your rights, sharing instead of explaining](grafiken/nutzen.en.svg){ .grafik }

- **A clear head.** You no longer have to remember deadlines, numbers and where things are. Ablago [reminds](eingang.md#erinnern) you of deadlines in good time and shows what's coming up.
- **Time.** You find the receipts for the tax return or the documents for the insurance in seconds – also in the text of the documents ([Search](ablage.md#suchen)).
- **Using your rights.** You claim the guarantee because the receipt is there. You deduct tradespeople's costs because the invoice can be found. You switch electricity supplier because you know when it's possible.
- **Sharing instead of explaining.** Your partner, the children or your parents look things up themselves instead of asking you – each exactly what they are meant to see ([Users and roles](benutzer-und-rollen.md)).

And your paperwork is **with you**, on your own device – not at a provider who analyses it.

## For whom it is especially worth it {#fur-wen-es-sich-besonders-lohnt}

![Families, own home, letting, parents, upheaval, small business](grafiken/lebenslagen.en.svg){ .grafik }

| Situation | What Ablago does there |
|---|---|
| **Couples and families** | The knowledge doesn't depend on the one person who is "the Library". |
| **Own home** | Maintenance, tradespeople, guarantees and proof over decades – for the house and every appliance. |
| **Letting** | Service charges, tenant changes and receipts for the tax in one place. |
| **Caring for your parents** | Powers of attorney, doctors' letters, care insurance and your parents' contracts – visible to all siblings. |
| **Upheaval** | Moving, building a house, inheritance, separation, retirement: if you have to touch everything once anyway, you might as well file it properly. |
| **Small business** | Holiday cover, deadlines, retention and receipts for the tax adviser: the knowledge belongs to the company, not to one person. |

!!! note "To be honest"
    If you live alone, rent, have few contracts and keep everything in your head, you may really not need a DMS – a binder on the shelf is enough then. Ablago is worth it as soon as **others** should know too or **a lot of time** passes between filing and needing.

!!! tip "And the paper?"
    After scanning, you may wonder what can go. Answers with rules of thumb are under [Keep or destroy the original?](original.md).

## Is Ablago something for me? { #ist-das-dms-etwas-fur-mich }

![From the household via the self-employed and small businesses to the large enterprise: what Ablago is built for](grafiken/personas-skala.en.svg){ .grafik }

Ablago is **built for home and for small businesses**. We have these people in mind:

- **Lena**, who does the paperwork in her family and has no technical knowledge. After an hour, her first folder should be created and the backup running.
- **Jonas**, her son, who finds his school report on his phone without asking.
- **Markus**, who likes tinkering, runs Ablago on his NAS and sets it up for his family.
- **Petra**, who looks after her mother with her brothers. Everyone sees the same paperwork, even from another city.
- **Mr Özdemir**, who owns two apartment buildings and wants to have the receipts for the tax year together.
- **Katrin**, who is self-employed and needs invoices, contracts and receipts for her tax adviser in one place.
- **a small team** of a few people who share mailboxes, deadlines and cover and must keep paperwork according to the rules.

For the self-employed and small businesses, Ablago already brings a lot: archive mode, procedure documentation, permissions per role, mailboxes, stand-ins, sign-in via a provider such as Microsoft 365, and automations. When in doubt, though, the rule is: what confuses a family, we don't build.

!!! note "What Ablago doesn't want to be"
    We **support** mid-sized companies, but don't build specifically for them. Ablago is not made for **large enterprises**: there you need things like approvals under the four-eyes principle, high availability or support with fixed response times. Ablago doesn't have them, and they aren't planned either.

All nine personas with pictures, and why we decided this way: [Who we build for](https://help.ablago.app/entwickler/aktuell/en/personas/) in the Developer manual.

## The 5-minute test {#der-5-minuten-test}

![Five documents you should be able to find in five minutes](grafiken/fuenf-minuten-test.en.svg){ .grafik }

Set a timer for five minutes and look for these five things. Even more revealing: ask someone from your family or your team to find them.

## Self-check: where do things get stuck? { #selbstcheck-wo-hakt-es }

Ten questions, answered honestly. There is no "pass" – only hints where a DMS can take work off your hands. Your answers stay in your browser; nothing is saved or sent.

<div class="selbstcheck" markdown>

1. Can you find the receipt for your most expensive household appliance within two minutes?
   *In Ablago the receipt is on the appliance's folder – with the purchase date and guarantee. A guarantee claim is then a matter of seconds.*
2. Do you know by when you can cancel your mobile phone or electricity contract?
   *If you enter the notice period on the contract, Ablago reminds you in good time – not to cancel, but so you can compare in peace.*
3. Does anyone besides you know which insurance policies you have and where they are?
   *In Ablago the people in your household look it up themselves – each exactly what they are meant to see.*
4. Do you know exactly what your home contents or liability insurance covers?
   *The policy with its terms is on the insurance's folder; the full-text search finds e.g. "bicycle" or "glass" in it.*
5. Are the receipts for the last tax year – tradespeople, donations, medical costs – in one place?
   *A view like "Tax 2025" collects all matching folders by itself, without you filing them twice.*
6. Could you say when the boiler or the car was last serviced – and by whom?
   *Boiler, maintenance contract, invoices and specialist firm are connected in Ablago; the next service is in it as a deadline.*
7. Can you find important proof such as the vehicle registration, school reports, certificates or vaccination record immediately?
   *A scan or photo in Ablago doesn't replace the original – but you always know what exists and where the original is.*
8. Do the people involved know where the powers of attorney or important agreements in your family are?
   *Ablago can make such documents visible specifically for certain people – for example for your siblings, if you look after your parents together.*
9. When moving, would you know whom you have to tell about the new address?
   *All contract partners are separate folders in Ablago – with customer number and contact. The list is already there.*
10. Can you name the customer number and contact person of your contracts without searching your email inbox?
    *Customer number, contact person and the last correspondence are right on the contract partner.*

</div>

If you chose "After searching" or "No" in several places, that's no failing – it's the same for almost everyone. A good start is usually **one** area: the insurance policies, the car or the tax receipts. How to create your first folder is described under [First steps](erste-schritte.md).
