Help and manuals

How can we help you?

Ablago is document filing for home and small businesses. Ablago is self-hosted, which means it runs on your own device, for example on your NAS, and not at someone else’s provider. Mappen instead of folders, connected by links.

You decide what leaves your filing system. No subscription, no telemetry, open formats. All six principles are on ablago.app; how Ablago handles data is described in Data protection in Ablago.
